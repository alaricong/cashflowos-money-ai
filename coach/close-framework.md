# Application Close Framework — Partnership with Alaric Mastermind

👉 This is the script the AI Sales Coach grades every call against. Edit it here.

## Opening frame
"So just to set expectations for how this call will go —
I'll start by asking you a few questions to understand where you're at, what your goals are, and what's been getting in the way.
Then, if I see a practical way to remove those obstacles, I'll walk you through what I'd recommend based on what you tell me.
If I don't think I can help, I'll be honest about that as well.
Does that sound fair?"

## 1) What result would you like to achieve?
1.1 What are the results you want to generate? In terms of monthly income or client acquisition?
1.2 Great — what is your target monthly revenue? (Let's get a real number. Is it roughly 10k, 30k, 50k, 100k/month?)

## 2) What is your current situation?
2.1 By the way (name), can you share what niche or industry you're currently focused on?
2.2 Roughly how much revenue are you generating a month right now? (Must get a number.)
- a) 0–5k
- b) 5k–10k
- c) 10k–50k
- d) 50k–100k
- e) Over 100k

2.6 How do you currently get clients? (referrals, ads, social media, DMs, webinars?)

## 3) Biggest obstacles (keep the digging loop)
3.1 What do you feel is the #1 biggest obstacle preventing you from going from [current situation] to [desired result]?
3.2 After they give you 1 obstacle: "What else is stopping you?"
3.3 "Okay, other than Obstacle 1 and 2 — any other bottlenecks you're facing right now?"

## 4) Biggest pain if nothing changes
4.1 What's the biggest pain point in your life or business if nothing changes after this call and you still can't hit [desired result]?
4.2 What's the worst-case scenario if this is not fixed?
4.3 How does it make you feel?

## Transition to solution (commitment AFTER you explain)
On a scale of 1 to 10 how willing are you to get your desired result?
"Awesome — based on everything you have said, I have the perfect solution for you. Can I share it with you?"

✅ Try to keep the pitch within 30 minutes.

## Recap the consultation questions
- Just now you mentioned you wanted to achieve (desired result), correct? (Wait for "Yes.")
- And you mentioned that currently, you're at (current situation), correct? (Wait for "Yes.")
- And you shared that your main challenges stopping you are (obstacles), right? (Wait for "Yes.")
- And your biggest pain if nothing changes is (pain), is that right? (Wait for "Yes.")
- Okay, based on everything you just said — I know exactly how to help you.

## "Deal or No Deal" frame (use to raise commitment, especially with indecisive prospects)
"Alright, here's how I usually do my calls. I'll walk you through everything that's included inside the Partnership with Alaric Mastermind, how it helps you hit your goals, and you're free to ask any questions you want during the call."
"But by the end of this call, I'd like you to make a decision. Either a 'Yes' or a 'No.' If it's a 'Yes' — awesome, we'll work closely together. If it's a 'No' — no worries at all, we'll still be friends."
"But if you genuinely need more time to think about it, you can just place a refundable $200 deposit. That locks in today's bonuses, and gives you a few days to make the final call."
"But I just want a decision by the end of the call — is that fair?" (Wait for "Yes.")
"Great! So just to recap — either a 'Yes', a 'No', or a $200 refundable deposit if you really need to think about it. Sound good?" (Wait for "Yes.")

## Qualification questions (pick a few depending on the flow)
- Do you feel like this program could help you? (If "Yes, I think so…" continue.)
- How do you feel this can help you? (Let them sell themselves.)
- So what makes you want to join the Partnership with Alaric Mastermind? (If hot: "Why do you want the program?" If warming up: "Why do you want to hit your desired result?")

## Handle logistics and soft objections BEFORE revealing the price
- Are you able to make a decision on your own? Or do you need to consult someone first?
  - If YES: "Awesome, that makes things smoother."
  - If NO (spouse/partner): "Totally understand — it's important to inform loved ones. But just to clarify — is it more of informing them, or do you need to get their permission before making a decision?" Informing = "___, I am going to sign up for this programme." Asking permission = "___, can I sign up for this programme please?" Which one are you?
  - If just informing: "Cool, so you're still the one making the final call, right?" (If not, offer a 3-way call or WhatsApp group with the decision-maker.)
- If this is the right fit, can you start now, or do you have to wait for a few months?
- Is there anything else — time, money, mindset — that could potentially stop you from doing this? (Dig. Uncover ALL objections now.)

## Soft-test affordability (before price reveal)
- If the price was $5000 — would that be something you could come up with?
- If they hesitate: instalment plan $500 x 12 months — manageable? If yes, get commitment to that plan, then go to "one last question, why do you want to succeed?"
- If they cannot afford, downsell: $1500 or $450 x 4 months.
- If still not, downsell: $500 or $150 x 4 months.
- Discuss payment options openly: credit card, instalment plan, borrowing, business loan, liquidation. Solve affordability BEFORE the price drop.

## Final emotional anchor
One last question… why do you want to succeed? (Let them speak their "why". Hot → why the program; warming up → why the result.)

## Price, objections & close (only once ≥80% sure they will buy)
Reveal: "So, the total investment for the Alaric Partnership Program is $5,000." (Pause.) "OR — if you need a payment plan — we do offer a 12-month instalment plan at $6,000. That's just about $16.40/day." (Pause.) "Would you be okay with that?"
- If "Yes, I'm in!": "Amazing! Let's get your spot secured now while we're on this call."

### Collect payment ON the call (always aim for during, not after)
- A) Pay in full: locks the spot, discounted rate ($5,000 vs $6,000), unlocks today's bonus: Done-For-You Webinar Funnel.
- B) Non-refundable deposit ($500 or $1,000): secures spot, can start using the program for 1 month, onboarding begins after minimum $500 paid; after 1 month if not continuing, can switch to the Facebook Accelerator course (valued $500).
- C) Refundable deposit ($100): can think 1 week or as long as needed; if they like it, continue; if not, switch to the Facebook Accelerator course (valued $500).

Payment instructions: "Great — here's my bank transfer / payment link. Go ahead and make the transfer now — I'll wait with you here on the call. Once done, I'll start your onboarding process. Sound good?" If they want to transfer after the call: "Let's just lock it in now while we're here — it'll just take 2 minutes."

## Handling objections & closing
Reconnect every objection to their goal, current situation, pain, and obstacles.
Frame: "I used to feel the same way..." or "There's actually someone just like you who said the same thing..." (then a testimonial / reframe).
Stay firm — most sales happen after the first objection. Persevere until a hard "No" at least 3 times (not maybes, not "I'll think about it").

### Legit concerns — answer, reassure, continue
- "I'm already in another program, will this clash with it?"
- "I have a product but not a high-ticket offer yet — will this still work?"
- "I'm not confident showing my face in videos or webinars."
- "I'll be overseas for 2 weeks — will I miss the coaching calls?"

### Excuses / limiting beliefs — agree, mirror, reframe ("Tai Chi" way)
- "I'm not very good at tech…", "I need to think about it.", "I have no money.", "I'm really busy right now.", "I'm not sure I can do this."
- Don't fight it. "I used to feel the same way…" / "Actually, there's someone in the program right now who said the exact same thing…" then how they overcame it.

### Handle objections BEFORE the price
10x easier before money is involved. The qualification stage handles time, energy, decision-maker and mindset objections, anchors pain and goals, frames the program as the bridge.

### Always link objections back to
Their current struggle, desired outcome, biggest pain, limiting belief, and the exact solution inside the Alaric Partnership Program.
Example — "I'm not sure I have time right now…" → "I totally get that. But you also said your biggest pain is not having a system to consistently attract clients. And that it's affecting your income and peace of mind. If nothing changes now… when will it change?"

## "I need to think about it"
Pre-empt at the start: "So here's how I usually run my calls… by the end of the call, I'll ask for a decision: either a 'Yes' or a 'No'… But I'd appreciate a decision today. Is that okay?"
- Option 1 — Clarify: "What specifically do you feel you still need to think about?" … "Before we hang up, are there any other concerns or unanswered questions I can help with?"
- Option 2 — Buying temperature: "On a scale of 1 to 10 — 1 'I wouldn't even do this if it was free', 10 'I'm ready to go right now' — where are you?" … "What would need to happen to move that to a 10?"
- Option 3 — Refundable deposit close: "How much time do you need?" ("3 days") "How about 7 days to be safe?" Then $500 refundable deposit secures spot, bonuses and current pricing.
  - Locks bonuses, discounted pricing, priority spot; 7 days to decide; refund immediately if no; top up remaining $4,500 if yes; check-in after 7 days; free resources meanwhile.
  - Why: "When someone puts down a deposit there's a 50/50 chance they move forward. If they don't put anything down, it drops to zero." Commitment turns follow-ups from chasing into service.
  - Optional time reframe: "Every day spent thinking about it is another day of delay… Money is renewable. Time isn't."
  - Final ask: "So are you okay with placing a fully refundable $500 deposit to hold your spot and bonuses for 7 days?" Make sure they transfer during the call; then congratulate, send onboarding + resources, book a follow-up within the 7 days.

## "I don't have the money right now"
First: real limitation or resistance?
- Option 1 — Clarity: "Are you saying you don't have the financial means right now? Or you do, but you're not ready to invest in this?" Real → financing/downsell/flexible plan. Has means → "What specifically makes you hesitant about taking action today?"
- Option 2 — Money range: "Roughly how much do you have access to right now? $0–200 / $200–1,000 / $1,000–5,000 / $5,000+." Above $1,000 → $500 deposit or custom plan; below → downsells, funding, resourcefulness.
- Financing options: $500 refundable deposit (30 days risk-free), $6,000 over 12 months (~$16.40/day), pay in full $5,000 + DFY Webinar Funnel, split payments (e.g. $2,000 today, rest next month), raise capital (bank financing, interest-free cards, family, pre-selling), use the program's AI tools for fast wins.
- Option 3 — Permission to dream: "Is this something you really want to do?" … "So how can we make this happen for you?"
- If they have money but won't commit: "Tell me more about that…" Anchor to their stated frustration and ask "What changes if you don't move forward?" … "How can we make this happen for you?"
- Option 4 — Cost vs value: "Do you currently have the $5,000?" If yes: "Does marketing / sales training / mastering funnels, ads and AI content cost you money or make you money?" If no: "Some people say 'I don't have the money' and stop. Others ask 'How can I find the money?' … Are you the kind of person who finds a way?"
- Committed but struggling: $500 refundable deposit today locks bonuses, pricing and access for 30 days — raise the full payment, test the program, or switch to a $500 program.

## "I'm too busy / I don't have time"
"We all have the same 24 hours — it comes down to priorities. Would it be fair to say the problems we discussed — [obstacles] — just aren't urgent enough to solve right now?"
- If YES (not urgent): "So you'd be okay continuing to live with [pains]?" "If I offered to solve all of them for free, would you jump on it today?" (If yes, the real objection is something else.)
- If NO (it is a priority): "What exactly is stopping you from moving forward today? Financial? Fear of another program not working?"

## "That's a lot of money — I'm scared"
"It is a big investment — I'd be worried if it didn't make you feel something. When I made big decisions I focused on the outcome, not the cost. What if this helped you create predictable income, position yourself as a leader, and automate sales?"

## "I've tried other programs — they didn't work"
"Did they have clients who got results?" If yes: "Why do you think they succeeded but you didn't — support, structure, accountability?" If no: "That's exactly what sets us apart…" — 20K+ course vault (AI, funnels, FB ads, branding, sales), live coaching & hot seats 2x weekly, blueprint onboarding, 24/7 WhatsApp support, tools to write ads/build funnels/close; act today and we build your webinar funnel.

## "There are cheaper options"
"Have you tried them?" Yes → "What results did you get?" No → "Why not?" Then: "If our program was the same price as the cheaper one, who would you pick?" ("You") "Why?" … "That's why we charge more — we deliver more, and clients get better results. Fair?"

## "Can I do it for free first, then pay after results?"
"If someone paid for your entire education, would you take it seriously — or more seriously if you'd invested thousands of your own? No skin in the game, no game. Are you ready to move forward today?"

## "I don't believe in credit cards"
"What concerns you most?" … "Used wisely, cards give fraud protection, cashback, points — and let you spread this over 12 months, sometimes interest-free. Some clients pay ~$16/day on a card and built 5-figure businesses in months. Open to it as a short-term launch pad?"

## Always end with value or rapport — buy or not
- Give value: "Even if you're not ready yet, here's a 3-step plan: 1) Craft 1 irresistible offer (solve 1 clear pain) 2) Set up a simple lead-magnet funnel 3) Use Facebook ads or AI content tools to drive traffic. Did that help?" (Alt plan: fix messaging; audit funnel/lead gen; one irresistible offer.)
- Build rapport: "As promised, I'll still send you the ad templates + webinar structure. Free." "How's business been lately? / How's the family? / How are things in [country]?"
- End with connection, not pressure. Make follow-up frictionless.
